Import projections / auction values (CSV or Excel)

Export a cheat sheet from FantasyPros, ESPN, RotoWire, Draft Sharks, etc. right before your draft and drop it here — .csv or .xlsx. Columns are auto-detected (player, team, position, auction value, projected points, and optional stat-level columns like pass/rush/rec yards & TDs — stat columns enable exact rescoring under your league's settings). ADP, ECR / consensus-rank, and Bye columns are detected too — a FantasyPros cheat-sheet export (100+ experts) drops in as-is, and its ECR drives snake availability. The legacy auc_values_ALL.xlsx sheet format imports directly, stat columns and all — keep building your sheet the way that's worked for you.

Keepers

Enter every kept player before the draft — yours and your rivals'. Keepers fill rosters and consume budgets like real picks, but don't advance the pick counter. In keeper auctions this is where the inflation edge comes from: cheap keepers leave more money chasing less talent, and the Adj$ column prices that in. In snake mode, your keepers reduce how many picks your plan uses (your latest rounds, as an approximation of forfeited picks).

How to run your draft with this app

  1. Before draft day: set your league's budget, roster and scoring in Settings. Import a fresh projections/values CSV in Data & News (the bundled 2026 set is a July consensus snapshot — refresh it the week of your draft). Apply boosts/fades for late news.
  2. Keeper leagues: enter every keeper (yours and rivals') in Data & News → Keepers before the draft. Keepers fill rosters and consume budgets without advancing the pick counter — and because kept players are usually below-market, the remaining pool inflates: watch Adj$ rise above AAV. That gap is your league's real price list.
  3. Pairing with your real draft: in Data & News → League sync, connect Sleeper (live auto-sync of every pick and price), ESPN (paste the league JSON), or Yahoo (paste the Draft Results text). Synced picks flow into the log exactly like manual ones — you can stop logging by hand entirely in a Sleeper auction.
  4. Every nomination (auction): type the player's name, click the row (or dot). The sticky bar shows the one number that matters — Max bid. Bid to it, never past it.
  5. Snake drafts: switch Draft type to Snake in Settings and set your draft slot. The board flips to pick-order thinking: market rank and expected round per player, an availability call against your next pick ("likely / risky / gone by then"), a Take-now urgency list ranked by how many points you lose by waiting, and a round-by-round projected plan. Log every pick (yours and others') so the pick counter stays true — or let Sleeper sync do it. Import a CSV with an ADP column for sharper availability; otherwise market values stand in.
  6. Every sale: record it — I won it or Someone else, with the real price. Everything (budget, inflation, values, optimal completion, max bids) recomputes instantly, and the draft log stamps each pick with how much of that position's market value is still on the board. The strip above the pool shows all positions live, and the "Pos left" column shows what would remain after each specific player goes — when RB drops under ~30% left, the tier cliff is on you.
  7. Targets and nominations: star (★) the players you want — they highlight on the board and the ★ filter chip shows just your list. Mark players you don't want with 📣 and they queue up in Nomination ideas, ahead of your DND players and the app's own suggestions. Money rivals burn on them is a discount on your targets.
  8. Strategy lab: toggle build archetypes — BBQ (two studs ~$115 combined, then ride the price crash), Stars & Scrubs, Balanced, Hero RB, Zero RB, Robust RB, WR Heavy, $5 QB, Wait on QB, Elite TE — to see how the model would finish your roster under each philosophy, with its own projection, Monte Carlo range, and when-to-use/risk notes. Definitions follow the published strategy literature. In auction they're price-tier constraints; in snake they're canonical round rules (no RB before R6 for Zero RB, QB after R8 for late-round QB, …). They update live, so mid-draft you can check "from here, is Zero RB still on the table?"
  9. Roster outlook (Monte Carlo): the floor/median/ceiling card simulates 1,200 seasons of your picks + the optimizer's plan, week by week, starting your best lineup each week — so boom/bust bench depth gets credit the averages miss. The block bar's "sim if won" line shows how a purchase moves your median and floor. Use it to steer roster shape (stars-and-scrubs vs balanced), not to sweat ±20-point differences — the ranges depend on variance assumptions.
  10. Trust the completion card: if it says your best roster needs a $34 WR tier-2, don't panic-spend $50 on tier-1. The plan updates as the room overpays (inflation > 0) or underpays.

Method details: see MODELING.md in the repo. The optimizer is an exact dynamic program equivalent to the original integer LP — provably optimal completion, re-solved in milliseconds after every pick. Values are VORP-based dollars blended with market AAV and corrected for live auction inflation.
