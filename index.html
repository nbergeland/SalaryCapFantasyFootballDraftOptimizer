How to run your draft with this app

  1. Before draft day: set your league's budget, roster and scoring in Settings. Import a fresh projections/values CSV in Data & News (the bundled 2026 set is a July consensus snapshot — refresh it the week of your draft). Apply boosts/fades for late news.
  2. Pairing with your real draft: in Data & News → League sync, connect Sleeper (live auto-sync of every pick and price) or ESPN (paste-sync). Synced picks flow into the log exactly like manual ones — you can stop logging by hand entirely in a Sleeper auction.
  3. Every nomination: type the player's name, click the row (or dot). The sticky bar shows the one number that matters — Max bid. Bid to it, never past it.
  4. Every sale: record it — I won it or Someone else, with the real price. Everything (budget, inflation, values, optimal completion, max bids) recomputes instantly, and the draft log stamps each pick with how much of that position's market value is still on the board. The strip above the pool shows all positions live, and the "Pos left" column shows what would remain after each specific player goes — when RB drops under ~30% left, the tier cliff is on you.
  5. Nominating: use Nomination ideas — expensive players you don't want. Money they burn elsewhere is discount on your targets.
  6. Trust the completion card: if it says your best roster needs a $34 WR tier-2, don't panic-spend $50 on tier-1. The plan updates as the room overpays (inflation > 0) or underpays.

Method details: see MODELING.md in the repo. The optimizer is an exact dynamic program equivalent to the original integer LP — provably optimal completion, re-solved in milliseconds after every pick. Values are VORP-based dollars blended with market AAV and corrected for live auction inflation.
